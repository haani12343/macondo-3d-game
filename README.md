# Into the Dark (Prototype)

**Into the Dark** is an early-stage prototype developed in Godot as part of my journey into 3D game development. This project serves as a learning environment where I can explore and understand the core systems required to build a complete first-person horror game. The current version focuses on experimenting with fundamental mechanics such as scene creation, 3D level design, player movement, camera controls, collision detection, lighting, and overall project structure within Godot.

The long-term vision for *Into the Dark* is a story-driven first-person horror experience set during a violent storm in a remote forest. The game follows a group of four friends who arrive at an isolated cabin in the middle of the woods to investigate a mystery. As they explore the cabin and its surroundings, they gradually uncover evidence of a secret cult that operates within the forest. Through environmental storytelling, clues, unsettling discoveries, and supernatural events, the player will learn more about the cult's rituals and the dark secrets hidden within the woods.

At its current stage, this repository is primarily focused on building and testing the technical foundations necessary for the final game. Development includes creating playable environments, implementing first-person controls, experimenting with interactions, and learning best practices for organizing a game project. This prototype allows me to gain hands-on experience with Godot while gradually developing the systems that will later be expanded into the complete horror experience.

Planned features for future development include:

* First-person exploration and interaction systems
* A detailed cabin and forest environment
* Dynamic weather effects, including rain and thunder
* Atmospheric lighting and sound design
* Story-driven objectives and clues
* Cult-related lore and environmental storytelling
* Horror events, suspense sequences, and scripted scares
* Character interactions and narrative progression
* Multiple gameplay outcomes and endings

This project is currently a work in progress and will continue to evolve as I learn new game development techniques and expand the scope of the experience. The prototype represents the foundation of a larger horror game concept and serves as both a learning project and the starting point for the future development of *Into the Dark*.
